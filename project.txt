## 1. CORE ARCHITECTURAL MANDATE
You are building a production-grade, neuro-symbolic multi-agent system—NOT a generic chatbot wrapper.
- **No LLM Math Hallucinations:** Never ask an LLM to guess future commodity prices, road distances, or spoilage percentages.
- **Deterministic & Foundation Model Spine:**
  1. Time-series forecasting MUST execute via `amazon/chronos-2` (or `autogluon/chronos-2-small` for CPU speed) using historical wholesale prices + 7-day Open-Meteo rainfall/temperature exogenous covariates.
  2. Spatial-thermodynamic arbitrage MUST execute via deterministic Python physics/economics functions using OSRM highway distances, diesel freight formulas, Arrhenius/Q10 thermal-humidity transit decay, and APMC mandi fee deductions.
  3. Policy & subsidy reasoning (NABARD e-NWR warehouse receipt loans, PM-FME cold storage grants) MUST execute via `HKUDS/LightRAG` (>= v1.3.9) in `mix` mode.
  4. Orchestration MUST run on `langchain-ai/langgraph` (v1.2.18) `StateGraph` with `MemorySaver`/`PostgresSaver` checkpointers and an explicit `interrupt()` human-in-the-loop gate before generating statutory PDFs or dispatching trucks.
  5. Data ingestion MUST run through standardized Model Context Protocol (`mcp`) tool servers wrapping `mandi-api.onrender.com` (keyless 5-state normalized API) and `api.data.gov.in` (OGD resource IDs `9ef84268-d588-465a-a308-a864a43d0070` and `35985678-0d79-46b4-9ed6-6f13308a1d24`).
  6. Multilingual Accessibility MUST pair `Bhashini ULCA` (voice ASR/TTS across 22 scheduled Indian languages) with `NakliTechie/Anuvaad` (`IndicTrans2-200M` ONNX via `onnxruntime-web`) running 100% inside the browser WebAssembly runtime for offline zero-server resilience.

Exact Mathematical & Scientific Formulas (engine/ Module Specs)Give these exact equations to Antigravity so the backend computes rigorous, defensible numbers during judge Q&A.Module 1: Covariate Time-Series Forecasting (engine/chronos_forecaster.py)Chronos-2 (amazon/chronos-2 at 120M parameters or autogluon/chronos-2-small at 28M parameters) uses a dual-attention mechanism alternating between Time Attention (across patches within a series) and Group Attention (across target price and weather covariates at the same patch index).   Input Tensors per Mandi $m$:Historical Target Price Vector: $\mathbf{y}_{1:T}^{(m)} \in \mathbb{R}^T$ where $T = 60\text{ days}$ (daily modal price in ₹/Quintal).Exogenous Weather Covariates: $\mathbf{X}_{1:T+H}^{(m)} \in \mathbb{R}^{(T+H) \times 3}$ where $H = 7\text{ days}$ (columns: precipitation_sum in mm, temperature_2m_max in °C, and a binary unseasonal_rain_shock flag).What-If Weather Shock Simulator Hook:
When a judge drags the "Rainfall Shock Slider" on the UI by $+\Delta R\text{ mm}$ (e.g., $+45\text{ mm}$ unseasonal cloudburst at the local mandi), the backend modifies future covariate slice $\mathbf{X}_{T+1:T+H}^{(\text{local})}$ in memory and re-runs Chronos-2 inference (which takes $<400\text{ ms}$ on CPU/T4 GPU).Output Quantiles:
For each day $h \in \{1 \dots 7\}$, extract the 10th percentile ($P_{10, h}^{(m)}$, downside panic-glut risk), 50th percentile ($P_{50, h}^{(m)}$, expected modal clearing price), and 90th percentile ($P_{90, h}^{(m)}$, upside shortage price).Module 2: Thermodynamic Transit Spoilage & Spatial Arbitrage (engine/spoilage_arbitrage.py)Standard apps assume 0% crop loss during transport. In reality, transporting perishable crops (Onion, Tomato, Potato, Green Chilli, Banana) in non-refrigerated open tarp trucks under high ambient heat and humidity causes rapid respiration and microbial decay.Q10 Biological Spoilage Decay Formula:
Let $T_{\text{amb}}$ be the average ambient highway temperature (°C) along the route, $RH$ be the relative humidity (%), and $t_{\text{transit}}$ be total transit + mandi queuing time ($t_{\text{transit}} = \text{duration\_hours} + 4.0\text{ hr queuing buffer}$).
The crop-specific spoilage rate constant $k(T_{\text{amb}}, RH)$ is modeled using the Q10 temperature coefficient ($Q_{10} \approx 2.2$ for perishables):$$k(T_{\text{amb}}, RH) = k_{\text{base}} \cdot \left(Q_{10}\right)^{\frac{T_{\text{amb}} - 20.0}{10.0}} \cdot \left(1 + \alpha_{\text{hum}} \cdot \max\left(0, \frac{RH - 70}{100}\right)\right)$$where $k_{\text{base}}$ is the baseline hourly loss rate at $20^\circ\text{C}$ (0.0018 for Onion, 0.0065 for Tomato, 0.0050 for Leafy Greens) and $\alpha_{\text{hum}} = 1.5$ (moisture rot multiplier).
The fraction of marketable weight lost in transit is:$$L_{\text{spoilage}}^{(m)} = 1 - \exp\left(-k(T_{\text{amb}}, RH) \cdot t_{\text{transit}}^{(m)}\right)$$Freight & Handling Cost Formula:
For a farmer or FPO shipping quantity $Q$ (in quintals, e.g., $Q = 50\text{ quintals} = 5\text{ metric tonnes}$ on a light commercial vehicle):$$C_{\text{transport}}^{(m)} = 2 \cdot d_{\text{km}}^{(m)} \cdot r_{\text{diesel\_per\_km}} + Q \cdot C_{\text{loading\_per\_qtl}}$$where $d_{\text{km}}^{(m)}$ is the OSRM highway distance, $r_{\text{diesel\_per\_km}} = ₹22/\text{km}$ (round-trip mini-truck freight equivalent), and $C_{\text{loading\_per\_qtl}} = ₹35/\text{quintal}$ (hamali / loading-unloading fee).APMC Market Fee & Commission Deduction:$$C_{\text{mandi\_fee}}^{(m)} = \gamma_{\text{APMC}}^{(m)} \cdot P_{50, h^*}^{(m)}$$where $\gamma_{\text{APMC}}^{(m)}$ is the state APMC market fee + commission fraction (e.g., 0.01 to 0.025).Net Realizable Profit per Quintal ($\text{NRP}_m$):$$\text{NRP}_m = \left[ P_{50, h^*}^{(m)} \cdot \left(1 - L_{\text{spoilage}}^{(m)}\right) \cdot \left(1 - \gamma_{\text{APMC}}^{(m)}\right) \right] - \frac{C_{\text{transport}}^{(m)}}{Q}$$Risk-Adjusted Downside Floor ($\text{NRP}_{m, 10}$): Computed identically using the 10th percentile forecast $P_{10, h^*}^{(m)}$ so the farmer sees worst-case guaranteed take-home cash.Module 3: Distress Crash Detection & LightRAG Policy Trigger (engine/lightrag_policy.py)Define the crop's Minimum Viable Threshold $P_{\text{floor}}$ (either the government MSP/MIS benchmark or the farmer's cost of production + $15\%$).Condition A (Spatial Arbitrage Wins): If $\max_m (\text{NRP}_m) \ge P_{\text{floor}}$, recommend the top-ranked mandi $m^* = \arg\max_m (\text{NRP}_m)$ and calculate exact Arbitrage Gain:$$\Delta \text{Gain} = Q \cdot \left(\text{NRP}_{m^*} - \text{NRP}_{\text{local}}\right)$$Condition B (Regional Crash — Trigger LightRAG Warehouse Subsidy Node): If $\max_m (\text{NRP}_m) < P_{\text{floor}}$ (meaning every reachable mandi within 250 km is crashing due to a macro glut), the LangGraph conditional edge routes automatically to LightRAGPolicyAdvisorNode.LightRAG ($\ge\text{v1.3.9}$, mode="mix") queries the indexed NABARD / WDRA policy graph to calculate an Electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipt (e-NWR) Pledge Loan:Pledge Advance: $75\%$ of current valuation at a WDRA-accredited scientific cold storage.Interest Rate: $7.0\%$ p.a. minus $3.0\%$ Interest Subvention under Kisan Credit Card (KCC) post-harvest extension = $4.0\%$ effective annual interest (just $0.33\%$ per month) while waiting 30 days for the mandi glut to clear.5. from typing import TypedDict, Annotated, List, Dict, Any, Optional
import operator

class MandiCandidate(TypedDict):
    market_name: str
    district: str
    lat: float
    lon: float
    distance_km: float
    transit_hours: float
    current_modal_price: float
    forecast_p10: float
    forecast_p50: float
    forecast_p90: float
    spoilage_loss_pct: float
    transport_cost_per_qtl: float
    mandi_fee_per_qtl: float
    net_realizable_price_per_qtl: float
    total_net_payout_inr: float
    route_geojson: Dict[str, Any]

class KrishiArthaState(TypedDict):
    # User Intake Slots
    farmer_name: str
    language_code: str                  # e.g., "mr_IN", "hi_IN", "as_IN", "te_IN"
    raw_query: str
    commodity: str                      # e.g., "Onion", "Tomato", "Potato"
    quantity_quintals: float            # e.g., 50.0
    farm_lat: float
    farm_lon: float
    farm_district: str
    weather_shock_Delta_mm: float       # Controlled by UI "What-If Rainfall Slider" (default 0.0)

    # Telemetry & Execution Trace
    execution_trace: Annotated[List[Dict[str, Any]], operator.add]
    mandi_candidates: List[MandiCandidate]
    optimal_mandi: Optional[MandiCandidate]
    local_mandi: Optional[MandiCandidate]
    net_arbitrage_gain_inr: float

    # Crash & Policy Graph State
    is_regional_crash: bool
    cost_floor_per_qtl: float
    lightrag_policy_recommendation: Optional[Dict[str, Any]]

    # Human-in-the-Loop & Final Output
    human_approval_status: Optional[str] # "PENDING", "APPROVED_DISPATCH", "APPROVED_ENWR"
    generated_pdf_url: Optional[str]
    vernacular_advisory_text: str
The 6-Node LangGraph Execution Topology (backend/agent/graph.py)intake_slot_node: Parses text or Bhashini ASR transcript into structured slots (commodity, quantity_quintals, farm_lat, farm_lon, language_code).mcp_telemetry_node: Invokes agmarknet_server.py and geo_weather_server.py over MCP to retrieve the 10 nearest mandis within a 250 km radius, their 60-day historical price series, OSRM highway geometries, and 7-day Open-Meteo weather forecasts.chronos2_forecast_node: Runs Chronos-2 across all 10 candidate mandis conditioned on the weather covariates (plus weather_shock_Delta_mm if the slider is active), outputting 7-day $P_{10}, P_{50}, P_{90}$ quantiles.spoilage_arbitrage_node: Executes the Q10 spoilage and OSRM diesel equations, sorts mandi_candidates descending by net_realizable_price_per_qtl, computes net_arbitrage_gain_inr over the local mandi, and sets is_regional_crash = (best_nrp < cost_floor_per_qtl).Conditional Edge (route_after_arbitrage):If is_regional_crash == True $\rightarrow$ transitions to lightrag_policy_node (queries HKUDS/LightRAG in mix mode for NABARD e-NWR warehouse loan calculation + cold storage directory) $\rightarrow$ transitions to human_interrupt_node.If is_regional_crash == False $\rightarrow$ transitions directly to human_interrupt_node.human_interrupt_node & pdf_execution_node:
Calls interrupt({...}) from langgraph.types to pause graph execution and send the recommendation card to the Next.js frontend.
